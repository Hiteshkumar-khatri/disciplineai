Install DisciplineAI on your phone

One scan, one tap — no app store, no downloads to manage.

👉 Scan this QR with your phone camera (or with Google Lens),
then follow the steps below for your phone.

📱 Android · Chrome
1

Open the app above (scan QR or tap Open DisciplineAI).

2

Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right) → Install app.

3

Tap Install. Done — the ⚡ DisciplineAI icon is added to your home screen and app drawer, like a real app.

📱 iPhone / iPad · Safari
1

Open the app above (scan QR or tap Open DisciplineAI).

2

Tap Share (the curved arrow at the bottom of Safari).

3

Tap Add to Home Screen → Add. The ⚡ icon lands on your home screen — tap it anytime to open the app full-screen.

ℹ️ Notes
i

All your data (tasks, streaks, coach chat) stays in this phone's browser — nothing is uploaded. It works offline too after the first visit. Want the same data on your computer? Each device keeps its own copy.
