Install DisciplineAI

Get it as an installed app — or just use it in your browser.

📱 Android
⬇️ Download DisciplineAI (.apk)
1

Tap the button above (or skip: open this page on your computer and scan the QR below).

2

Tap the downloaded DisciplineAI-1.2.0.apk file.

3

Android will say "not allowed to install unknown apps from this source" → tap ⚙️ Settings → Allow apps from this source → go Back.

4

Tap Install → Open. The ⚡ icon now lives in your app drawer, like a real app.

🌐 Just want to use it now? Open web app
📱 iPhone / iPad / other

iPhone can’t install app files — Apple only allows apps from their App Store. The one-tap way on iPhone is adding it to your Home Screen (opens full-screen like an app):

1

Open the app: https://hiteshkumar-khatri.github.io/disciplineai/

2

In Safari tap Share (⤴) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

3

The ⚡ icon lands on your Home Screen — tap it anytime to open the app full-screen.
